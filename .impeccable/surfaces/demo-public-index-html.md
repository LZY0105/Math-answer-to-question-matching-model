## Direction contract

Build a calm evidence workbench where every answer is earned, reviewed, or refused. The visual world is editorial paper, navy ink, mathematical notation, fine rules, and a continuous evidence ledger—not SaaS chrome. A visitor chooses one of three safety paths, inspects the exercise and answer PDFs, follows the central decision trace, and verifies the evidence below. The first viewport must hold document identity, all three scenario controls, both sources, and the verdict together. The form is a split-document desk joined by a narrow decision spine. The Canva reference contributes topology, palette, restrained density, and the central trace; its tiny type, excessive empty space, decorative grid, and generated copy must not be reproduced.

## Component grammar

Hairline rules join related regions. Corners stay nearly square. Elevation is reserved for the complete workbench and the matched excerpt, never repeated as a card system. Serif display type names the evidence; compact sans-serif and monospaced labels carry operations. Status always combines a word, icon, and color.
